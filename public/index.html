The House

Designed with families in mind

Tucked into a quiet, soundside Nags Head community, Short Stack is a classic beach box made for families, whether you're here with little ones, teens, grandparents or the whole crew. Upstairs opens into a bright living room and a fully stocked kitchen and dining area. The lower level, refreshed in 2025, adds a private fourth bedroom with its own bath, a rec room with a pool table and kitchenette, and a full-size washer and dryer.

You're right between the beach and the sound: an 8-minute walk to the ocean, with a soundfront community pool, playground, pickleball court and boat ramp in the neighborhood, and favorite OBX towns, attractions and dining close by.
